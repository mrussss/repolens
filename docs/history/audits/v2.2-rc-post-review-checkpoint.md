## Fixed in this checkpoint

- Ownership-lost/finalized Runs no longer strand the losing Attempt in
  `RUNNING`. The handler closes the Attempt independently with
  generation/status fencing, diagnosis workers emit Attempt heartbeats, and
  the worker process runs `RecoverySweeper`. Expired job leases cannot be
  renewed after expiry.
- Diagnosis polling retries transient status/report/steps failures with
  bounded backoff, surfaces recoverable errors, and reads steps at most once
  per polling cycle.
- Migration 009 is resumable across MySQL's implicit DDL commits. It serializes
  migrators, verifies existing columns/indexes before continuing, uses
  idempotent backfill, and refuses duplicate Attempt identities without
  deleting legacy rows.
